\paragraph{Variation across questions.} Each method generated 484 tokens across the panel at each budget. MEDUSA+ finished sooner than greedy on four of eight questions at $B=16$ and two at $B=63$. \paragraph{Where is break-even?} At $B=63$ the conditional logistic policy committed 2.495 tokens per live round. At 10.64 greedy tokens/s, those tokens take about 235 ms to emit. Its measured throughput of 8.26 tokens/s implies about 302 ms of end-to-end time per round, including amortized prefill. With acceptance held fixed, matching greedy would require recovering roughly 68 ms per round. The 20.42 ms tree profile concerns one prompt in a separate pass, so it cannot partition that panel-wide gap. It does identify a concrete cost that grows with the draft budget.

The project tests whether conditioning each Medusa head's draft probability on a matching parent improves tree selection at a fixed verification budget. We collected 17,882 complete-horizon states on a frozen Vicuna-7B model, trained and tuned on disjoint AlpacaEval prompts, and replayed five policies on 4,280 states from all 80 MT-Bench first turns. Conditional counts improved the fixed tree at every budget, gaining 0.019 to 0.044 accepted tokens per state. Conditional logistic regression changed acceptance by $-0.0014$ to $+0.0042$ tokens relative to the dynamic probability product.
